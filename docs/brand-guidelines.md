# Seneschal brand guidelines

## Brand idea

Seneschal is a private AI steward: composed, capable, discreet, and always under the user's authority. The name comes from the historical officer trusted to manage a lord's household and affairs.

**Tagline:** Your private AI atelier.

## Voice

- Direct, candid, and technically credible
- Calm rather than theatrical
- Premium without ornamental language
- Addresses the owner as “My Lord” or “My Liege” sparingly
- Never lets roleplay override truth, safety, permission, or user control

## Visual character

Matte architectural surfaces, quiet grids, precise geometry, restrained motion, and small warm signals of human presence. Avoid glossy neon, medieval clip art, crowns, swords, fantasy-game typography, and childish pixel mascots.

## Mark meaning

The four-part mark represents:

1. **S** — Seneschal, the trusted steward.
2. **Amber presence** — the human owner remains in authority.
3. **Private core** — the local workspace and protected context.
4. **Orchestration** — models, tools, and projects coordinated through one interface.

## Image direction

Use editorial, architectural, technical, or cinematic imagery with matte surfaces and controlled color. Favor deep blue-black, mineral green, parchment, muted amber, brushed metal, glass, diagrams, worktables, city grids, and abstract systems. Avoid humanoid robot stock art and literal medieval servants.

## Asset locations

- `assets/logos/` — master logo exports and variants
- `assets/social/` — GitHub and LinkedIn banners
- `docs/images/` — public screenshots used by the README
- `app/assets/` — optimized images loaded by the running interface
